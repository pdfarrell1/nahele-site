Ordering questions

How do I order a piece?

Send us the piece name through the form above, email, or Instagram DM. We'll confirm it's still available (every bloom is one of a kind), then arrange payment and shipping. Most orders ship within a few days.

Can you preserve flowers from my own event?

Yes — lei, wedding bouquets, graduation haku, and more. Message us before your event if possible: blooms preserve best when they're fresh. We'll walk you through how to get your flowers to us.

Are the flowers really real?

Always. Every petal is a real flower, gathered at peak color, dried slowly, and sealed by hand in a crystal-clear finish. That's why no two pieces — even of the same design — are exactly alike.

How do I care for my jewelry?

Keep it dry, apply perfume and lotion first, store out of direct sunlight, and handle gently. The full guide lives on our care page — a care card also comes tucked in every box.

Where can I see pieces in person?

We pop up at craft fairs and markets across the islands. Upcoming dates are always posted on Instagram — come say aloha and see the blooms up close.
